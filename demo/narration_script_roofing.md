## Before each take
- **Booking scene:** book as "Jessica Moreno" with the email `gilbeydulnuan+live@gmail.com`. After recording, delete her contact and deal yourself.
- **Voice agent:** call from a phone you can control. The slot must show on the calendar and in the CRM, and the whole scene is the riskiest, so record it twice.
- **Invoice scene:** open INV-3106 (Robert Sanders, $16,700, unpaid) and use Send. Allow about 10 seconds for the PDF.
- **Telegram scene:** press Start on the bot using the link for Chloe Nguyen's contact (open the Telegram button on her card), so your own Telegram account acts as Chloe.
- **Numbers:** say only numbers you've checked on screen after loading the demo. The dashboard totals also include your existing data, so they won't match the seed's $186,050 pipeline and $46,000 won. Use the deal values above, and read dashboard figures from the screen.

## Don't say
- Anything about SMS or WhatsApp (not working).
- "Instant." Say "about thirty seconds" or "within seconds".
- Pricing or guarantees.
